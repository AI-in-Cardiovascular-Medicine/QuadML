# Getting started — overview

## What QuadML is

QuadML lets you build, compare, and explain machine-learning pipelines on tabular data without writing code. It covers four tasks—classification, regression, survival analysis, and clustering—and searches every combination of the preprocessing steps and models you select, then reports metrics, plots, and explanations. Everything runs locally on your computer; no data is uploaded.
